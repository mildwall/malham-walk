Appleby: things to tick off (11:13 – 13:07)

In walking order: a loop of about 3 km and 40 min of actual walking, from the station down to the river and church, up Boroughgate to the castle gate, then back. Tap an item to tick it; ticks are saved on this phone.

Turn round by 12:40 wherever you are. The walk back up to the station is uphill and takes about 15 min, and you want to be on the Leeds-bound platform by 13:00. Opening times are from the places' own websites and guides, not confirmed for this Sunday.

Walks from Ribblehead station (13:47 – 16:44)

A · Viaduct close-up and back ≈3 km · 1 h

From the station go down to the road junction by the Station Inn, then take the wide gravel track north-west across the moor to the foot of the viaduct (24 arches, about 400 m long). Come back the same way. Flat and easy; the track can be muddy.

Safe whenever: you're never more than ≈20 min from the platform.

B · Viaduct to Blea Moor signal box ≈6–7 km · 1 h 45 – 2 h

As A, then continue on the path that runs beside the railway on its east side, north to Blea Moor signal box below the tunnel mouth. Turn around there and come back the same way. Gentle rise, rough in places, boggy after rain. Whernside fills the view on the left on the way out.

Turn around at the signal box by 15:30 at the latest (the page's "Leave by" tile works this out from GPS). That gets you back in the last daylight with a margin.

Not today: Whernside

The well-known circuits over Whernside or round Blea Moor are 13–17 km and take 4½–6 h. They don't fit between 13:47 and sunset at 16:35.

Tickets and practical notes

  • Ticket: an Off-Peak Day Return Shipley ↔ Appleby (via Skipton), quoted at £29.50 for this date, ⅓ off with a railcard. Ribblehead is on the route, so getting off there on the way back should be a normal break of journey. Ask the conductor or ticket office to confirm before you get off.
  • Appleby wait (1 h 54 min): see the tick list above. The town centre is about 10–12 min downhill from the station. Turn round by 12:40.
  • Seats: going north, a seat on the left is often recommended for views down Dentdale. On the way back the Appleby–Ribblehead section is in daylight too.
  • Weather: one model forecast (TheWeatherOutlook) gives about 9 °C, light wind (gusts ~17 mph), no rain, 94 % humidity under high pressure. That means a dry day but possibly low cloud or mist on the tops. Ribblehead is at about 300 m, so it will feel colder than Shipley. Take a hat, gloves, waterproof and a torch: the 16:44 leaves after sunset.
  • Sunday trains in the North West: Northern has been cutting some Sunday trains because conductors are short. Check the live boards below on the morning, and again at Appleby before 13:07.
